# Togie Valley - phone QA checklist
1. Open the hosted URL over https; the title screen appears with no error panel.
2. Install to the home screen (Android Install / iOS Add to Home Screen); icon and name look right.
3. Launch from the icon: fullscreen, no browser bar, notch/safe areas respected.
4. Enable airplane mode, kill and relaunch: the game loads (music may be silent).
5. Fonts (Baloo 2 titles) still render offline.
6. New Game: start in the house, chest arrow pulses; open chest.
7. Tap to walk; double/triple tap runs/sprints; hold to steer.
8. Rotate the phone portrait <-> landscape: HUD and camera adapt.
9. Pinch zoom and two-finger twist work; no page scroll or pull-to-refresh.
10. Hoe, plant, water, sleep; crops grow next day.
11. Shop: buy seeds; locked items show why.
12. Ship crops in the bin; morning summary pays out.
13. Fish: cast, bite, reel minigame, catch; notebook unlocks the entry.
14. Cook a dish at the stove; buff shows.
15. Rain/snow weather looks right; frame rate stays smooth.
16. Sound: sfx, ambience, music, Settings volume/mute persist after relaunch.
17. Low-end test: after sustained lag the look simplifies (no halftone) and play stays smooth.
18. Background the app 1 min, return: game resumes, no audio glitch.
19. Autosave: close the app mid-day, reopen -> Continue restores position and time.
20. Export save code, New Game, Import code: progress restored.
21. Lock screen / incoming call during fishing: no stuck state on return.
22. Battery/heat after 15 min of play is acceptable.
